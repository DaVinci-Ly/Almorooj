About us

An industrial company specialised in wheat milling and feed manufacturing, run on clear written rules.

Background

We started in 2022 with a clear idea

Al Morooj Al Khadra for Flour and Feed Mills was founded in Qasr al-Akhyar to be a source the bakery and the farm can both rely on. We chose to build the company on a single rule: the specification is written and known, and delivery happens on time.

We run two parallel lines — milling wheat in its various grades, and manufacturing compound feed — serving bakeries, distributors, poultry producers and livestock farmers.

What we stand for

Three rules that govern our decisions

Our mission

To supply flour and feed at a consistent specification and a fair price, and to give customers the same clarity in dealing with us as they find in the product.

Our vision

To be the first choice for anyone looking for a dependable supplier of flour and feed — known by name, not by price alone.

Our values

We do not sell what we would not accept ourselves. The specification is not lowered under pressure, and mistakes are fixed rather than explained away.

Quality

How quality is controlled in practice

Quality here is a chain of checkpoints. It starts before the grain comes in and ends after the bag is sealed.

  • Incoming shipments are tested for grain cleanliness, moisture and impurities.
  • Samples are drawn during the run and compared against the approved specification.
  • Every batch is numbered and its record kept, so it can be traced if needed.
  • Packing, weight and sealing are reviewed before the shipment leaves the store.
