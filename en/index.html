How we work

Four stages between the grain and the bag

Each stage has an owner and a checkpoint, and no batch moves on until it passes.

  1. Intake & inspection

    The shipment is weighed and sampled, then tested for cleanliness and moisture before it is accepted.

  2. Cleaning & sifting

    Grain passes through sifting and separation stages that remove impurities, stones and broken kernels.

  1. Milling & sieving

    Wheat is milled in stages, then sieved to reach the fineness required for each product.

  2. Packing & storage

    Products are packed in sealed bags and held in a dry, prepared store until the delivery date.
