Our products

Three production lines: wheat flour, semolina and bran, and compound feed.

Line one

Wheat flour

Wheat is milled in stages and then sieved to the fineness required. We offer three grades covering bakeries, pastry kitchens and home use, each with an approved specification we hold to on every run.

  • Premium flour: white and fine, for white bread, pastry and confectionery.
  • Wholemeal flour: higher extraction rate, retaining part of the bran layer.
  • Bakery grade: prepared for continuous daily production in bakeries.
Raw material
Wheat selected after inspection
Packaging
Sealed bags — sizes as agreed
Suited to
Bakeries · Distributors · Retail
Line two

Semolina & bran

Semolina and bran come out of the same milling stages, each with its own specification. Bran is separated and processed so it is clean and fit for feed use.

  • Fine semolina: for pastries and dishes that need a firm texture.
  • Coarse semolina: for couscous and traditional dishes.
  • Wheat bran: clean and sifted, used in livestock and poultry feed.
Source
Output of the milling and sieving stages
Packaging
Sealed bags — sizes as agreed
Suited to
Restaurants · Pastry producers · Farms
Line three

Compound feed

We produce balanced feed blends from ingredients of known origin, matched to each animal type and growth stage. A custom formulation can be agreed if you have a specification of your own.

  • Poultry feed: blends for layers and broilers across their stages.
  • Cattle feed: formulations for dairy cows and beef cattle.
  • Sheep feed: blends for fattening and general flock care.
Formulation
Standard, or to customer specification
Packaging
Sealed bags — sizes as agreed
Suited to
Poultry farms · Livestock farmers · Distributors
Common questions

Before you order

Can you produce a feed blend to our own specification?

Yes. Send us the specification, we review it with you, then agree the formulation and quantity before production starts.

What bag sizes are available?

Bag sizes vary by product and order volume, and are agreed when the quote is prepared.

Do you provide a sample before ordering?

Yes, a sample of the requested product can be supplied for evaluation before any agreement.

How is delivery handled?

The delivery date is agreed when the order is confirmed, and we hold to it. For repeat orders a recurring supply schedule can be arranged.
